University of Houston AGL download edition of Marmousi-II

Gary S. Martin, Robert Wiley and Kurt J. Marfurt
Allied Geophysical Laboratory, University of Houston, TX, USA
Model Definition downloads: https://www.agl.uh.edu/downloads/downloads.htm
Data access and terms: https://www.agl.uh.edu/resources-data.php

Martin, G. S., Wiley, R., and Marfurt, K. J. (2006). Marmousi2: An elastic
upgrade for Marmousi. The Leading Edge, 25(2), 156–166.
https://doi.org/10.1190/1.2172306

We sample Vp, Vs and density on a 68 × 200 grid (depth × distance) with 45 m
spacing. The last nominal depth of 3515 m samples the source boundary at
3500 m. We smooth Vp with a 180 m Gaussian and apply the study's lower bound
based on fixed Vs. Vs and density stay fixed during inversion. Material
arrays use float32 and coordinates use float64. Velocities are in m/s,
density in kg/m³, and coordinates in m.

The three AGL source archives are downloaded by the preparation script. The
training, truth and combined NPZ packages are generated locally. SHA-256
values for the archives and prepared data appear in protocol.json and
manifest.json respectively. Keep this attribution with derived work.
