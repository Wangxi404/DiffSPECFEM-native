Marmousi-II model data

The University of Houston Allied Geophysical Laboratory lists the Marmousi II
Dataset under Level 3 – Limited. Consult the provider's terms for your use.
https://www.agl.uh.edu/resources-data.php
https://www.agl.uh.edu/downloads/downloads.htm

The software obtains the three Model Definition archives directly from AGL
and prepares the model on the user's machine. The repository's software
license applies to code. Model inputs remain subject to AGL's data terms.
